# Electronic Gun Model Design Plan

## Purpose
完成させる電子サバゲー用の銃3Dプリンタ/改造用モデルの設計図および部品表。

## Target Specifications
- **Length**: ~159 mm (barrel + grip)
- **Barrel Diameter**: ~80 mm outer, ~72 mm inner (4 mm wall)
- **Grip Width**: ~35 mm at widest, 3 mm wall
- **Material**: PLA (3D printable)
- **Print Settings**: 0.2 mm layer height, infill 20%, supports on overhang > 45°
- **Electronics**: LaserX reference (iFixit, YouTube) + hobby store components

## 3D Model
- **File**: `2026Bunkasai_gun.stl` (ASCII STL, unit: mm)
- **Generated by**: `gen_stl3.py`
- **Geometry**:
  - Barrel: length 120 mm, outer Ø80 mm, inner Ø72 mm (4 mm wall), capped both ends
  - Grip: tapered hollow, 55 mm long, 35 mm wide at widest, 3 mm wall
  - Barrel axis along +Z (0→120); grip extends rearward from Z 100→158.5
- **Mesh stats** (verified):
  - Triangles: 288
  - Unique vertices: 147
  - Bounding box: 80 × 80 × 158.5 mm

## Slicer Settings
- **File**: `slicer_settings.json`
- **Layer height**: 0.2 mm
- **Infill**: 20% (grid pattern)
- **Supports**: enabled for overhangs > 45°
- **Estimated print time**: ~2.5 hours
- **Estimated filament**: ~200 g PLA

## Mechanical Components (3D Printed)
| Part | Description | Approx. Dimensions | Print Time |
|------|-------------|-------------------|------------|
| barrel | Hollow cylinder, capped both ends | 120 mm × Ø80 mm (4 mm wall) | ~1.5 hrs |
| grip | Tapered hollow grip (rear of barrel) | 55 mm × 35 mm (3 mm wall) | ~1 hr |
| nozzle_housing | Laser/emitter holder (optional) | 20 mm × 10 mm | ~0.5 hr |

## Electronics (non‑printed, reference only)
- **Laser module**: LaserX (ifixit.com link)
- **Control**: Arduino/ESP32 (laser‑tag projects referenced)
- **Power**: 3.7 V Li‑Po or AA batteries
- **Mechanical mounting**: screws into barrel inner wall

## Assembly Notes
- Design for two halves (barrel + grip) that snap‑fit or use M3 screws.
- Include mount points for PCB (outside barrel) and battery holder.
- Optional: integrated trigger mechanism from toy gun modification (100‑yuan source).

## Bill of Materials (BOM)
- PLA filament: ~200 g
- M3 screws (10 mm): 6 pcs
- M2 screws (5 mm): 4 pcs
- 3D printer consumables (support material)

## References (from obsidian/first.md)
- iFixit LaserX teardown
- Scribd LaserX Ultra Double Blasters instructions
- YouTube: "LaserX Ultra Double Blasters" (00:01:01)
- Arduino/ESP32 laser‑tag projects (duino4projects, Sand1929, Hackaday)

## Next Steps
1. Refine STL with proper wall thickness and mounting holes.
2. Generate printable slicer settings (Cura/PrusaSlicer).
3. Test print prototype.
4. Integrate LaserX module and control electronics.
5. Finalize documentation and commit to repository.
